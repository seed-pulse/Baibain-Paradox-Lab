# 🌰 Baibain Paradox Lab
**栗まんじゅうは宇宙を埋め尽くせるのか？**

A public, reproducible thought-experiment laboratory for *Baibain*: exponential replication, matter/energy accounting, causal reach, self-gravity, and hypothetical safety interlocks. Initiated 2026-11-18 (JST).

## Research question
The fictional gadget doubles a treated object **every five minutes**. Given a 50 g bun and continued duplication, what can physics constrain, and what must be specified about a fictional replicator before claiming it stops or remains safe?

**Key distinction:** destroying a bun ≠ proving the replication mechanism has stopped. A fictional machine could preserve or transfer its replication authorization through changes of matter, so its operational semantics must be defined separately from thermodynamics.

## Evidence standard
- **CANON**: explicitly supported by an identifiable source. The five-minute doubling interval is stated by [TV Asahi](https://www.tv-asahi.co.jp/doraemon/story/0135/) and [its later synopsis](https://www.tv-asahi.co.jp/doraemon/story/0497/). Canon does **not** establish a hidden permit/energy mechanism.
- **MATH**: exact consequences of stated assumptions (e.g., `N=2^n`).
- **MODEL**: computed proxy requiring additional physical approximations (uniform-density sphere, Newtonian pressure, etc.).
- **SPECULATIVE**: invented safety mechanisms or extrapolations beyond evidence.
